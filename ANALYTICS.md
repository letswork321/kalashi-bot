# Calculation, timing, provenance and validation contracts

## Non-negotiable execution boundary

Only public market-data GET requests, optional authenticated market-data WebSocket subscriptions, and Telegram message POST requests exist. There are no account, balance, order, cancel, or fund endpoints. Strategies and the simulator cannot place real orders. Chrome receives authenticated read-only analytics, never API keys. Live collection never falls back to synthetic prices; `--mock` remains a separate fixture/database and does not start the intelligence worker or send alerts.

## Time and locked tracker

All stored timestamps are UTC epoch seconds. Display/grouping uses IANA `America/New_York`, including daylight-saving transitions. `tzdata` supplies the IANA database on Windows.

Every discovered binary KXBTC15M market with a 15-minute window gets one registry row and one lock record. The official prediction target is **market open +60 seconds**, with a strict deadline of **+65 seconds**. The first valid fresh forecast in that window locks UP when baseline p_up >=0.5, otherwise DOWN, regardless of the economic NO TRADE threshold. Quote event time must be no more than 2 seconds old; the forecast must have been calculated at/after the target. No valid observation by the deadline => MISSED. Decisions cannot be rewritten: SQLite enforces immutability of lock time, state, features, model, side and probability. Only the official finalized result is reconciled later.

A market discovered after the deadline, including offline history/backfill, is marked MISSED with a reason. It is never given a retrospective prediction. Counts cover discovered eligible markets only, not unknown never-listed windows. Today/7d/30d/all views include pending/missed counts, accuracy, consecutive settled win/loss streak and paginated access to every stored market. Preliminary next-market outlook is only the latest EMA trend; no probability or official-strike assumption is invented. It is not scored as a lock.

Kalshi KXBTC15M resolves YES when its closing CF Benchmarks BRTI 60-second average is at least its opening average, per the market's `rules_primary`. The price to beat is Kalshi's official `floor_strike`, never a local Coinbase approximation. Coinbase is a spot venue, not the settlement index. A result is accepted only from a finalized/settled market with explicit `yes`/`no`; closed time, spot and expiration_value do not substitute for the official result. Official settlement timestamps also constrain when labels may be used for training.

## Independent engines

Each pure function runs in its own task in a three-worker pool, with the same snapshot; no function consumes another engine's output. Regime does not suppress any strategy. Missing required data yields an explicit insufficient/warming-up state, not a partial confluence score. Performance is distinct confirmed signal events versus official outcomes; signals in the same market are correlated, not independent trials. Unconfirmed watches do not enter the confirmed-engine accuracy denominator.

### Breakout

Uses consecutive completed UTC 15-minute Coinbase candles. EMA20 is SMA-seeded, then alpha 2/21. The latest completed candle's LOW must exceed both its closing EMA20 and the VWAP as of its exact close; its CLOSE must exceed the highest HIGH of the preceding 25 completed candles (excluding the trigger candle). Current spot must be >=current VWAP. The trigger close must be no later than the current market opening: a closed-bar confirmation is information for the next market, never retroactively applied to the finished one. Developing price beyond the last 25 highs while above VWAP is only UNCONFIRMED BREAKOUT WATCH.

VWAP is **sum(actual trade price × actual trade size) / sum(actual trade size)** with a UTC-midnight session anchor. Its weighted standard deviation uses the same real trades. Trade IDs are paginated/deduplicated and contiguous coverage intervals certified; a missing interval invalidates VWAP. Closing VWAP excludes trades at/after the closing boundary. No typical-price × candle-volume proxy is substituted. A bounded initial backward trade-ID backfill attempts 32 pages of real prints toward UTC midnight. COMPLETE/PARTIAL/FAILED jobs are recorded. If the cap is reached or continuity fails, VWAP remains warming up. Optional `python -m intelligence.cli backfill-trades --max-pages 200` extends that bounded attempt. On a first partial day it can remain warming up until complete session coverage is recorded. Keep the collector running through the next UTC midnight; real coverage from that anchor is needed before these gates can pass.

### Reversal / Leaning Farmer

Bollinger Bands use the 20 completed candles BEFORE the actual market opening: arithmetic mean ±2 population standard deviations. The single observation target is **open+600 seconds**. Public REST cannot promise a continuous-time measurement at exactly a mathematical instant: the first BTC trade event in [target,target+2s], received by target+3s and no more than 2s old, is accepted and its actual event time is stored. All other/late samples are MISSED; neither candles nor later prices reconstruct it. Zero-width bands are insufficient. Crossing upper => possible DOWN fade; crossing lower => possible UP fade.

Measurable reversal outcome: at **open+840 seconds** (14 minutes), a similarly on-time spot quote must move >=0.02% toward the band mean from the +10m price. Missing outcome observations are excluded, not declared wins. This spot-reversion event is not a guaranteed Kalshi contract win. Probabilities remain PROBABILITY NOT VALIDATED until >=100 genuine outcomes exist and the last chronological 30% contains >=30 test observations. Then the panel reports the untouched holdout frequency and Wilson 95% interval, not a guaranteed forecast. No historical OHLC backfill manufactures exact 10m quotes.

### Seven-indicator confluence (all seven required)

1. EMA27/63: +1 if fast >slow, -1 if fast <slow; equality neutral. This is the current crossover regime, not a claim of a new crossing every poll.
2. Wilder RSI14: <=30 =>+1; >=70 =>-1; otherwise 0.
3. Last completed bar volume >=1.5×mean of preceding 20 bars: +1 on bullish candle body, -1 bearish, flat 0. Below threshold =>0.
4. MACD12/26 with EMA9 signal: positive and increasing histogram =>+1; negative and decreasing =>-1; otherwise 0.
5. Acceleration: difference of the two adjacent 25-second log-return velocities, scaled per minute²; >=+0.00005 =>+1, <=-0.00005 =>-1.
6. Tick velocity over the last 50 seconds: >=+0.0001 log-return/min =>+1, <=-0.0001 =>-1. Requires >=10 consecutive trades spanning at least 50 seconds and latest age <=3s.
7. Current spot deviation from verified trade VWAP in weighted standard deviations: >=+1 =>+1, <=-1 =>-1; otherwise 0. Zero/unknown deviation =>insufficient data.

Score >=+6 =>CONFLUENCE BUY; <=-6 =>CONFLUENCE SELL. Five votes never qualifies. Bullish RSI and bullish trend conditions may disagree by design; no thresholds are relaxed to create alerts.

## Radars and executable economics

YES buy asks are complements of NO bids; NO buy asks complement YES bids. Sells use same-side bids. Depth is walked for the full quantity, not assumed from a midpoint. Default slippage is $0.002/contract on each leg, taker fee coefficient 0.07 × effective series/event multiplier, total position cost+fee rounded up to $0.0001. The official fetched schedule has no settlement fee. Multi-level average-price fee estimates can differ slightly from per-fill exchange rounding. Unknown fee schedules suppress baseline estimates.

Flip Radar uses >=3 observations spanning >=20s from the same market, observed bid velocity, spread/depth, live BTC context and remaining time. It presents the executable round-trip loss NOW and a separate CONDITIONAL future bid target, its required movement and after-cost scenario. Target movement is at least 3¢ and at most 15¢ over 60s. Potential opportunity requires positive measured velocity supporting that movement, scenario net >=1¢, spread <=8¢ and >=120s remaining. WATCH/NOT ECONOMIC/STALE are explicit. 56¢ has no special status. Targets are extrapolations with no calibrated success probability.

Scalp windows are 20/40/60s. REST explicitly reports FASTER MARKET DATA REQUIRED. Optional sequenced Kalshi WebSocket books must be fresh <=1s and have >=20 distinct update timestamps in the last 20s; quote/quality/depth gates still apply. Positive extrapolated net >=1¢ with spread <=2¢ and sufficient remaining time yields OPPORTUNITY; otherwise WATCH/AVOID. This is conditional opportunity detection, not proof of reliable 20s profits. Historical trade prints/OHLC cannot certify executable scalp exits.

Fair value uses a candidate only after >=200 locked samples, >=60 disjoint OOS test predictions, ECE <=0.10 and Brier/log-loss no worse than the preserved baseline on the same test rows. It displays expected probability minus executable ask, slippage and taker fee. Until then it says INSUFFICIENT DATA. All candidates stay shadow-only. The initial candidate is trained on the early lock horizon; later-in-market feature vectors can be out of distribution and must remain insufficient until horizon-specific evidence exists. Inputs beyond five training standard deviations fail the fair-value validation gate. Passive/maker fills are never assumed; absent queue/fill evidence, maker-versus-taker comparison is explicitly unsupported rather than presented as executable savings.

## Paper simulator

Legacy baseline paper history is untouched. New directional, flip and scalp ledgers are separate. A signal creates a pending intent; no immediate fill. Default execution delay is 2s; an actual fresh, depth-sufficient book received at/after that deadline must arrive within 3s. Otherwise the entry is cancelled. Timed exits similarly require an actually observed bid, not a future maximum/high, with both taker fees and adverse slippage. Missed exits remain unresolved open risk, excluded from closed-return metrics and prominently flagged as incomplete performance. Directional positions held to settlement use only official outcomes. Passive orders are not simulated as filled. Open cost and unresolved exits are shown alongside closed P&L/ROI/drawdown; these are hypothetical, never real money.

## Research and models

Public backfill: current settled Kalshi markets, documented historical archive/cutoff, bounded samples of Kalshi minute bid/ask candles, and <=300-candle Coinbase 15m windows. Use `python -m intelligence.cli backfill --days 7|30|90 --max-pages N`; jobs preserve requested windows, actual row counts and COMPLETE/PARTIAL/FAILED outcomes. Initial worker does a bounded 7-day backfill (10 pages/window segments). Larger windows require explicit CLI backfill. A completed HTTP window does not prove the exchange has no missing intervals. Candle charts are not historical order books; past depth and realistic past scalp fills are unavailable. Raw trades/books are collected forward.

ET weekday/hour tables provide actual outcome frequencies, candle volatility/absolute returns/volume, logged spreads/depth/flip moves and sample counts, plus individual strategy/regime outcomes and closed simulator P&L. Hourly aggregates keep long histories out of the page-render loop. Data gaps remain null. Descriptive weekday/hour frequencies are NOT validated predictive time effects.

Training is implemented, not implied by logging. The original heuristic is retained unchanged as baseline. A standard-library deterministic L2 logistic candidate uses distance to official strike, minutes remaining, volatility, momentum, acceleration (with a missing-tick indicator), EMA gap, RSI, relative volume, implied probability and imbalance. Only genuine on-time locked feature vectors with official labels are eligible; neither historical proxy strikes nor today's reconstructed features are used. Normalization is fit on training rows only. Training/evaluation uses the most recent 240 settled locked markets at most, bounding laptop CPU costs; prior version audits remain stored and overlapping test windows across model versions must not be pooled as independent samples. Minimum fold: 60 expanding train markets, 20 later validation markets, 20 later test markets. Hyperparameter choices (0.1/1/10 L2) and Platt-style calibration use validation only. Official label availability must precede later decisions. Test blocks are disjoint; no shuffled split. All fold timestamps/tickers and predictions are retained in versioned SQLite artifacts.

The background worker retrains after at least 100 settled locks and >=10 new samples (checked every 30 minutes). CLI `python -m intelligence.cli train` triggers the same real fit. Live shadow predictions are stored alongside immutable locks, not substituted for baseline. The evaluation center reports accuracy/Brier/log-loss/calibration, regime/day test groups, quote-to-settlement after-fee proxy returns, live shadow metrics and version history. Proxy returns are not the delayed executable simulator ledger. There is no automatic baseline promotion or promise that a new candidate improves profitability.

## Optional data and alerts

REST requires no Kalshi key. Optional backend-only WebSocket transport is disabled by default. Install `requirements-ws.txt` only if desired. Explicitly set `KALSHI_WS_ENABLED=true`, `KALSHI_DATA_KEY_ID`, and `KALSHI_DATA_PRIVATE_KEY_FILE` to a local PEM file for a data-reading credential. The code signs only GET `/trade-api/ws/v2` and subscribes only to `orderbook_delta` with explicit legacy NO-leg pricing. It never calls trading/balance APIs. Sequence gaps, negative quantity, rollover and reconnect invalidate the book until a fresh snapshot arrives. Auth, headers, keys and socket exception details are never sent to Chrome or logs. Credentials are never requested in chat/GitHub. The network must allow HTTPS/WSS to `api.elections.kalshi.com`. If authentication/key provisioning is unavailable, REST stays operational and the scalp limitation remains explicit.

Existing Telegram directional/outcome messages remain. Extra categories are disabled by default, controlled on the backend via `INTELLIGENCE_ALERT_CATEGORIES` (comma-separated `breakout,reversal,confluence,flip,scalp,settlement,daily`; strong directional forecasts already use the existing sender). Additional categories have persisted unique keys, expiry, 5-minute category/market cooldowns and a six-message/hour cap, backoff and redacted failures. A stale or mock observation cannot queue a live opportunity. Ambiguous network acceptance can still cause an at-least-once duplicate.

## Retention and resource use

SQLite WAL, indexed event times and short writes separate the collector, three CPU-light strategy workers and research/model worker. Rendering reads snapshots; heavy training is never done in a Flask handler. Raw ticks target 2 days with bounded cleanup batches; raw books target 7 days and full snapshots 2 days. Books referenced by paper entries/exits are retained for audit even after raw retention expires. Radar history stores state changes and 30-second heartbeats for 30 days, avoiding duplicate per-second rows. Optional backend variables INTELLIGENCE_TICK_DAYS, INTELLIGENCE_BOOK_DAYS and INTELLIGENCE_SNAPSHOT_DAYS accept 1–90 days. Candle/outcome/lock/model/paper and ET-hour aggregates remain. A busy BTC feed can produce substantial disk use; monitor the data folder and keep backups. Retention never invents coverage after deleting trades. All tracker markets are available through pagination; detailed simulator browser history is capped at 200 rows/category while aggregate statistics include the selected range.

## Tracker/alert patch (supersedes earlier scheduling and alert descriptions)

An independent forecast clock polls at 200ms around the +60s target. Separate short-timeout public GET workers refresh authoritative metadata, completed 1m candles, BTC quotes and Kalshi books. The clock never waits for those HTTP operations or research/fee/settlement/Telegram calls. On-time freshness: BTC event <=2s, book receipt <=2s, request latency <=2s, metadata <=30s. Original baseline math is shared unchanged through `forecast_components`; fee availability does not suppress accuracy forecasts. Missing/invalid input attempts are logged. +65s remains the on-time deadline. A first valid observation through +120s is recorded in a separate immutable LATE table, with actual time/strike/BTC/model/features, never added to on-time accuracy/training. Old MISSED rows are not changed. The dashboard displays both cohorts and exact failure reasons.

The clock records baseline feature vectors without claiming an unverified fee or entry quote; quote-return proxies are unavailable for those rows. Real paper and Telegram-ledger fills remain observed delayed execution studies.

Flip cards show per-side executable asks/bids, both costs, liquidity, movement required and conditional target net. Each live status change/30s heartbeat with cost inputs starts an independent one-contract forward event study: observed ask after 2s, observed bid 60s after entry, both fees and $0.002/leg slippage. Fresh quote/depth required within a 3s deadline; missing entry/exit stays missed. Target touches are observations, not assumed target fills. Overlapping studies are explicitly not a portfolio. Side comparisons never select the largest baseline probability. Historical support requires >=100 nonoverlapping completed potential-opportunity studies, >=30 observations in the final chronological 30%, positive normal-approximation 95% lower bound for mean net, and no missing observations; historical support is not current positive expected value or guaranteed return.

Settings live in SQLite, not a secrets file; existing Basic authentication plus same-origin JSON and process-bound CSRF protect changes. Defaults: locked forecasts, potential directional entries, withdrawals and official outcomes ON; preliminary/flip/scalp/engine/daily additions OFF unless explicitly configured. Saved switches override environment defaults and survive restarts. A dedicated dispatcher sends alerts independently of research. Forecast/outlook/outcome messages do not consume the six-per-hour opportunity budget; each forecast/outlook has a unique market key. Engine/flip cooldowns 300s; scalp cooldown60s per side/horizon. Scalp TTL3s, flip/entry TTL10s. Queued evidence is rechecked; quote movement >0.5c, stale data, sequence loss or reversal invalidates opportunities. Failed short-lived sends are not retried beyond expiry. API acknowledgement timestamp is recorded after POST completion; phone latency/read status is not observable, and ambiguous network acceptance can still cause a duplicate on retry.

Preliminary alerts are EMA/momentum-only, with fresh quote and an explicit unknown-strike warning, in the 60-to-30s window before next open. Locked forecast alerts explicitly label the heuristic uncalibrated and not an entry. Potential directional alerts use validated, in-distribution calibrated shadow estimates, >=5% after-cost edge, <=4c spread, >=5 top-five entry contracts, >=120s remaining, <=2s quote/book ages and <=1s request latency. No STRONG-label requirement; heuristic percentages alone do not qualify. Legacy uncalibrated-entry outboxes are superseded after migration; existing Telegram connection and official result logic remain; legacy outcome messages are forwarded into the single deduplicated dispatcher so the two senders cannot race. Delivered directional opportunities receive one bounded withdrawal/expiration notification.

Scalp fast-data gates: authentic sequenced current-ticker WebSocket book; >=20 distinct events in last20s spanning >=10s; maximum gap2s; last event, exchange latency, BTC quote and latest contiguous BTC trade <=1s; real trade-volume/velocity/acceleration. Both UP/DOWN independently evaluated for20/40/60s. Entry candidate requires conditional net>=1c, spread<=2c, time>hold+20s, signed BTC velocity>=0.0001/min, recent5s bid velocity>0, no signed acceleration below -0.00005/min² or negative recent-bid reversal, signed imbalance>=-0.2. Until per-side/horizon nonoverlapping forward shadow samples satisfy >=100 closed/30 final holdout, positive lower mean-net bound and zero unresolved exits, candidates are downgraded to WATCH. Only the unchanged fixed-rule candidates generate shadow paper research, to avoid changing the rule to manufacture validation. A conditional future bid is never treated as available liquidity.

Alert evidence is SQL-immutable at enqueue. A dedicated network-free execution observer polls cached scheduler/live inputs every200ms so slow research cannot block subsequent fill observation. It requires recently verified same-market fees; stale or missing quotes are still never substituted. Repeated observer polls cannot reuse displayed size from the same book event. Timed paper evaluation begins only on the first supported live observation within3s AFTER Telegram API acknowledgement, followed by another2s execution delay. Fills require later observed asks/bids and depth, never prices preceding acknowledgement; late delivery/missing observations/capacity are recorded honestly. Alerted directional results use official settlement and separate after-fee net. Legacy sent messages without original timestamps are not reconstructed. Performance aggregates use all recorded notifications within the selected range; only the latest100 audit rows are sent to Chrome.
