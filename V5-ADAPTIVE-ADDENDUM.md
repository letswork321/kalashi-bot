## Threshold definitions and forecast independence

Efficiency = absolute net log-price move / sum of absolute one-minute log-price moves over 20 returns. Trend: efficiency>=0.55; ranging<=0.35; chop<=0.25 with adjacent nonzero return sign-change frequency>=0.45. Reversal-heavy: frequency>=0.60. High volatility: most recent 10-return SD / prior 40-return SD>=1.5 (or genuine emergence from zero prior variation). Low volume: most recent five-candle mean / preceding twenty-candle mean<=0.60. Transition: short efficiency breaks away from or collapses against earlier efficiency, or a substantial short move opposes the preceding direction. Flags can coexist.

Public BTC depth is context, not proof of hidden liquidity or cancellation intent. Relative thin/high depth uses <0.5/>1.5 of the preceding snapshot median after 20 observations. It is source/receipt-time quality gated. Kalshi YES/NO odds/book/favorite are still excluded from the forecast; executable contracts are compared afterward only.

All checkpoint windows, preservation rules and manual-only trading requirements remain unchanged. These adaptations produce an experimental paper opinion, not a calibrated 75% claim or automatic entry/sell instruction. Learning whether adaptations work requires future settled rounds; 100 non-missed 15-minute rounds take at least 25 hours, and weekday/regime conclusions require substantially broader coverage.

A separate bounded two-second observer now publishes descriptive current regime context even between checkpoints. The dashboard labels that context separately from immutable checkpoint opinions and checks both observer age and original reference-event age before calling it fresh. This observer cannot create checkpoint predictions or Telegram alerts. Network inputs, live context, the forecast clock, research and delivery each run outside one another's blocking paths.
