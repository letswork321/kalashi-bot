## Scoring and economic definitions

A path hypothesis is sampled prospectively on BOTH sides once per market-minute, whether favorable or not. Ask/quantity/assumed fee/goal/stop/time/version/provenance are immutable. Entry executes hypothetically on an observed ask after 2s with 0.002 modeled adverse slippage; fees are recomputed for that actual delayed entry. Exit uses sellable bid/depth with the same modeled slippage and taker fee. Goal, stop or market-end barrier occurs first. Missing data or inadequate depth is UNVERIFIED. This is conservative discrete observed-book evidence, not a guarantee every exchange event was delivered or a real fill occurred. No passive order fill is assumed. Correlated minute samples are not independent wins; first-passage training selects one predeclared comparable sample per market.

Original PATH v3 retains its original 3s continuity/ack rules and all historical records. New V4 caller ENDPOINT means a fresh price observed between acknowledged+180 and +182s meets the original frozen spot target. It does not assert a continuously observed path or mix denominators with PATH. New warning endpoints mean a >=0.05% directional move at issued+60..62s. Flat/missing/no-call states are separate; a post-event move is not hindsight evidence of an earlier call.

Manual realized results are supplied by the user, never account verified. Estimated entry fees remain labeled in realized results. Profit targets and observed target touches are not promises of realized earnings. Default standard taker fee estimates are qualified until current applicable fee metadata is verified.
