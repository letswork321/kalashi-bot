## Forecast arithmetic and uncertainty

Completed 1m log returns estimate the last 60-minute sigma. Log(reference/strike) divided by the final-minute-average noise scale anchors the BTC-only forecast. Bounded short-lived drift (at most 0.35 sigma/minute, horizon capped at 3 minutes) combines explicit contributions in `features.drift_parts`. These fixed weights are a new transparent experimental model, not learned/validated coefficients. A 99% raw estimate can still be wrong and is not calibrated win likelihood. Guidance remains WAIT while predictive calibration/after-fee value is unproven; favorable contract prices do not alter direction. Missing optional flow/frames makes conviction LOW rather than suppressing the basic forecast.

The new trained candidates are research-only: reporting does not silently replace the fixed model. Multiple checkpoints within a market are correlated and never pooled as independent markets for superiority claims. Same-market superiority requires adequate forward samples and paired discordance evidence; a newer version alone is not an improvement.

## Resource / provenance bounds

Lifetime scoreboard uses full retained history (not capped). Score aggregates refresh at most every 10s; current original/latest fetch independently. Browser history is capped at 80 compact records; forensic research at 10,000 settled checkpoint rows with scope disclosed, training at the last 500/checkpoint. Paper drawdown is labeled as the last 2,000 entries in issue order, not a settlement-time equity guarantee. No forecast-history retention deletion exists. No balance/order/portfolio execution endpoint is introduced. Optional data keys remain backend-only and disabled by existing explicit security/opt-in controls.
